🎭 Wildmask

Every island is different. You're a field researcher dropped on a wild procedurally-formed island: sunny plains, scorching desert mesas, murky swamps.

Study the animals — sneak close (crouching helps!) and hold E to fill their research meter. A fully-studied animal grants its DNA and is sent to the zoo at your camp, where guests pay coins to visit.

Craft masks from DNA at your field tent ⛺ and transform into animal hybrids: 🐸 jump & kick · 🐴 gallop · 🐊 swim · 🐭 shrink · 🦂 climb & venom. Each form opens up terrain — and habitats — you couldn't reach before.
